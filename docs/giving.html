Merici

Merici is my primary cause. Merici is dismantling global child trafficking through a proven effective process, including rehabilitation of the victims. I have spent a fair amount of time with the founder, Om, who has dedicated his life to this mission. Eliminating this pure evil and giving these children a second chance helps to alleviate trauma that holds back future generations. At this point my role is philanthropy through investment and collaboration.
