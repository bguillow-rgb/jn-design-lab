The story

About

I grew up outside Albany, New York, in a town nobody has heard of. I learned the value of hard work early on through a combination of manual labor as a landscaper and construction worker and mechanic, as well as buying and selling cars in my parents' driveway starting at 15 years old.

The first car

At 14 I worked summers at my uncle's car repair shop, doing the grunt work. The next summer he told me to buy an abandoned 1989 Honda Civic a customer had left behind. It cost $35, the scrap value. I put about $200 into tires, a battery, and an exhaust, cleaned it up, and sold it through a newspaper ad for $900. That was a moment for sure, because I rolled it into another car, and then another. By senior year of high school I had a BMW and $20k in the bank while my friends were borrowing movie money from their parents.

Lemons into lemonade

After college I started a dealership with a friend. I knew better, I ignored my gut, and eleven months later he skipped town and left me about $100,000 in the hole. Over the next four years I rebuilt from nothing into a former Chevy dealership facility, a dozen employees, 40-plus cars a month, and a top-rated seller account on eBay, before Carvana existed.

The actual In Demand Auto commercial, 2014.

ACV Auctions

ACV started on a yellow legal pad: notes and hand-drawn sketches of a 20-minute online auction, born out of my own frustration with losing whole days at physical auctions. We launched in Buffalo in 2015. That October we won the 43North competition and its $1 million first place prize, six days after my daughter was born. From there we grew from 50 cars a month to more than 50,000, raised roughly $300 million of venture capital, and went public on the Nasdaq in March 2021 at a valuation over $5 billion. My eight years there were many lifetimes of experience condensed into a whirlwind pressure cooker.

DealerClub

In 2023 I started again. DealerClub was the wholesale auction rebuilt around reputation, so honest sellers get rewarded for being honest. Cars Commerce acquired it three months after we went live commercially.

“Cars are not commodities. They aren't bushels of wheat.”

Joe Neiman

Trust and transparency, the slogan I have carried since the In Demand Auto days, was the same idea DealerClub was built on in 2023.

The health story

I'm an open book on this one. Ulcerative colitis showed up at 19 and stayed with me, on and off, for more than 15 years. I ran down every rabbit hole there is. My experience and my truth is that my disease was driven by excessive stress and anxiety, which is the space I lived in secretly for most of my life. Until I actually learned how to identify that and manage it and restore balance to my body, nothing would work. I have actually experienced instantaneous healing, where active symptoms resolved themselves overnight after I was able to reframe my disease in my mind. I'm not a doctor and this isn't medical advice. It's just what happened to me, and it changed how I build, work, and live.

“Everything needs a soul. People are not robots.”

Joe Neiman

Flying

In five years I went from zero hours to more than a thousand, and a commercial instrument multi-engine certificate. The two extremes of my hangar say it all: an open-cockpit Waco biplane and a Piaggio Avanti. Flying my family from Florida to New York in three hours, above the airliners, feels like a superpower. Being a true adult student taught me more about self-discipline than any business ever did.

Any fool with too much money can buy a Bugatti, but you're still stuck driving from one traffic light to the next.

What I read
I've read hundreds of books in my adult life. I try to be a student of lives well lived, and try to apply the lessons learned to my life.
