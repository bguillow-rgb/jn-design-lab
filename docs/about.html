The story

About

I grew up about 20 minutes outside of Albany, New York, in a pretty forgettable rural town. I learned the value of hard work early on through a combination of manual labor as a landscaper and construction worker and mechanic, as well as buying and selling cars in my parents' driveway starting at 15 years old.

The first car

At 14 I started working summers at my uncle's small car repair shop, doing all the grunt tasks around the shop. The following summer he told me to buy an abandoned 1989 Honda Civic a customer had left behind. The price was the value of the car in scrap metal, which was $35. I spent about $200 putting used tires, a battery, and an exhaust on it, cleaned the car up, put an ad in the paper, and sold it for $900. That was a pivotal moment. I kept flipping cars out of my parents' driveway throughout the rest of high school, and by senior year I had a nice BMW 3 series and $20k in the bank, while my friends had to borrow $20 from their parents to go see a movie.

Lemons into lemonade

After college I moved back to Albany and started a dealership with a friend from high school. I knew better, but I ignored my gut feeling, and eleven months later he skipped town one night and left me about $100,000 in arrears. I had no choice other than to turn lemons into lemonade. Over the course of about four years I rebuilt myself and my business from $100,000 in the hole into leasing a former Chevy dealership facility, a dozen employees, selling 40-plus cars per month, and being a top rated seller on eBay.

The actual In Demand Auto commercial, 2014.

ACV Auctions

ACV started on a yellow legal pad filled with notes and hand-drawn renderings of what a 20-minute online auction could look like, born out of the pain points and inefficiencies I had lived with as a dealer sourcing cars at physical auctions. On June 1, 2015 we ran our first auction in Buffalo, a high-mileage Mazda Tribute that drew bids from three local dealers and sold for around two thousand dollars. That was the start of something big. That October we won the 43North competition and its $1 million first place prize, when my daughter was just six days old. We grew the company from three cofounders to over 2,000 teammates, raised approximately $300 million of venture capital, and went public on the Nasdaq in March of 2021 at a valuation over $5 billion. I consider my eight years growing ACV to be many lifetimes of professional careers, condensed into a whirlwind pressure cooker.

DealerClub

In 2023 I started again with DealerClub, the wholesale auction rebuilt around reputation, where honest sellers get rewarded for being honest. Cars Commerce acquired it three months after we went live commercially.

“Cars are not commodities. They aren't bushels of wheat.”

Trust and transparency, the slogan I have carried since the In Demand Auto days, was the same idea DealerClub was built on in 2023.

The health story

I'm an open book on this one. The colitis started at 19 and continued on and off for more than 15 years. I ran down every single rabbit hole conceivable to try and resolve it. My experience and my truth is that my disease was caused by excessive stress and anxiety, which is the space I lived in secretly for most of my life. Until I actually learned how to identify that and manage it and restore balance to my body, nothing would work. I have actually experienced instantaneous healing, where active symptoms resolved themselves overnight after I was able to reframe my disease in my mind. I'm not a doctor and this isn't medical advice. It's what happened to me.

“Everything needs a soul. People are not robots.”

Flying

In the past five years I have gone from zero to 1,000-plus hours, flying highly complex aircraft, with a commercial instrument multi-engine pilot certificate. I own multiple airplanes, but the two extremes are a Waco YMF-5 biplane and a Piaggio Avanti P180. Flying my entire family from Florida to New York in three hours, at altitudes above the airliners, feels like a superpower. Being a true adult student has taught me a lot about self-discipline, and I take it seriously and respect the risks and decision making that come with it.

Any fool with too much money can buy a Bugatti, but you're still stuck driving from one traffic light to the next.

Joe Neiman
