# joeneiman.com - positioning and design references
For Kurt. Compiled 2026-10-16 from Joe's own material (website answers, autobiography, PD deck, texts) and live site reviews done the same day.

## How Joe wants to be positioned (his words)
"Real and accessible and relatable. An average guy who has been able to achieve some pretty incredible outcomes." Not a guru: "it can't be a self-promotion ego-trip... It has to add real tangible value to other people's lives." Stories carry the lessons. The site's jobs: bring him deals (franchise dealerships, planes, early-stage health/marketplaces/transportation), hold the giving and advisory record, host the writing later, let people reach him.

He named one site he loves: Rich Roll's ("great website").

## Closest positioning matches (reviewed live 2026-10-16)
1. neverenough.com - Andrew Wilkinson. Exited founder, memoir about wanting more and learning what's enough. Candid self-deprecating bio list ("Lit $10 million on fire", "watched my stock drop 80%"). Warm dark cream palette, real photography, serif type. CLOSEST overall match in both positioning and tone.
2. jesseitzler.com - Marquis Jet founder. Family/fitness/adventure/fun framing, almost Joe's own "family, fitness, flying, fun" slide. Press logos + real trip photography. Leans merch/program-heavy; Joe's version should not.
3. richroll.com - Joe's named favorite. Health transformation + storytelling + podcast + books, dark editorial design, photography-led.
4. sive.rs - Derek Sivers. The anti-guru extreme: plain text, "Me in 10 seconds," radical accessibility (personal email answered). Tone reference more than design reference.
5. charliepgarcia.com - the model Joe admires for distribution. Credential-forward, flag hero, gold/cream. His positioning (authority) is the opposite of Joe's (relatable); useful mainly as contrast.
6. dealershipguy.com - Joe's industry world; media-brand layout, not a personal site. Context only.

## Best non-AI design references
- neverenough.com - the whole package: typography, warmth, candor. Steal the self-deprecating bio-list device.
- jamesclear.com - cleanest entrepreneur-to-author architecture ever shipped: one sentence, book, articles, newsletter, speaking. The structural model for where Joe's site goes once the book exists.
- jimmychin.com - full-bleed real photography doing all the work, tiny amount of text. The model for Joe's aviation imagery (Waco, Avanti, cockpit shots, the uncle's floatplane story).
- richroll.com - editorial dark theme done by humans, strong art direction.
- sive.rs - proof that plain and personal beats polished and generic.

## What reads as AI-generated (avoid)
Purple-blue gradient heroes, identical rounded cards with icon+title+blurb, emoji section markers, centered everything, stock photography, "Innovator. Investor. Visionary." triads, perfectly parallel feature grids.

## Direction to propose
Never Enough's warmth and candor + James Clear's architecture + Jimmy Chin's photography treatment applied to Joe's real images (Porsche shoot heroes, hangar and cockpit shots, the floatplane origin photo pending approval). First person throughout, Joe's own sentences from the questionnaire as the raw copy. A candid "who I am" list in Joe's voice instead of an accomplishments wall.
